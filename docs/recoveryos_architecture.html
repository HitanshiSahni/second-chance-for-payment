Layer 4 · Tier 2 Boundary Learned ML Intelligence & NIR Economic Optimization
app/ml/ · app/services/action_evaluator.py · action_router.py
feature_engineering.py
51 Causal Features
Single source of truth: 23 base features + 24 category×action interactions + 4 fatigue/health modifiers.
data/model.joblib
Logistic Regression S-Learner
Pipeline(StandardScaler, LogisticRegression L2, C=1.0). ROC-AUC 0.7695, Accuracy 74.85%, ECE 0.98%.
action_evaluator.py
Counterfactual Lift (ΔP)
Scores P(Recovery | Action) vs P(Recovery | WAIT) baseline. Eliminates unneeded interventions with zero lift.
action_router.py
NIR Revenue Maximizer
NIR = (ΔP × Amount) - Cost. Selects max positive NIR action; gracefully falls back to WAIT if NIR ≤ 0.
▼ Selected Optimal Action Dispatched
